
 In 2009, «OKTAPODI», an animation film
produced and created by students at
GOBELINS, was nominated for an Academy
Award (Oscar) in the category of Best Animated
Short Film in Hollywood. «Oktapodi» had
already received numerous awards from some
of the most prestigious animation festivals
around the world:
- SIGGRAPH Asia 2008 – Jury Award
- SIGGRAPH Los Angeles 2008 – Best of Show
and Audience Prize
- Hiroshima 2008 – Special International Jury
Prize
- Annecy 2008 – Canal+ Family Award
- Anima Mundi – Rio de Janeiro 2008 – Best
Student Film and Audience Prize
- FICCI BAF 2008 – India – Best Student
Animated Short Film
- Imagina – Monaco 2008 – Best Animation
Award.
