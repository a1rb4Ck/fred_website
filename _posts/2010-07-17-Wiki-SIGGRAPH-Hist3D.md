[Pierre Hénon](https://spatialmedia.ensadlab.fr/equipe-20122013/equipe/pierre-henon/) from les [Arts Déco Paris ENSAD](https://www.ensad.fr) / [ENSADLab](https://www.ensadlab.fr) has published a huge research, interviews and bibliographical work in the Wiki [SIGGRAPH Hist3D](https://histoire3d.siggraph.org/) on the french 2D and 3D animation history, from 1980 to 2000.  
With others contributors: [hist3d.fr/presentation/equipe](https://hist3d.fr/presentation/equipe/)  

He has organised and numerised some of [my work](https://histoire3d.siggraph.org/index.php/Fr%C3%A9d%C3%A9ric_Nagorny) with the [Relief studio](https://histoire3d.siggraph.org/index.php/Relief), and uploaded some of my shorts on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yh1zAhopspw), like [Le Pantin](https://histoire3d.siggraph.org/index.php/Le_Pantin).

Many thanks to him!  
I heard that a big book should be published on the topic in the near future.
